Christopher (Chris) Rafter

Owner & Senior Consultant, WhiteSquirrel.ai

Christopher (Chris) Rafter has spent his career where data, AI and the day-to-day of running a business meet. He is co-founder and COO of Inzata Analytics, a data analytics and semantic modeling company. There he works across product strategy, sales, partnerships, pricing, hiring and client delivery.

He is the mind behind SIGNLD.ai, Inzata's decision intelligence platform. SIGNLD connects a company's business systems and files into a single knowledge graph, so leaders can ask questions in plain English and get answers in minutes instead of waiting days on a spreadsheet. Every number links back to the record it came from, so answers can be checked rather than taken on faith. It connects read-only to more than 800 business systems and keeps metrics consistent across sources. It also keeps an eye on the questions a business cares about most and flags when the answers change. It's built for operators, not analysts, and holds SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA and GDPR certifications.

He has applied AI and analytics to small and mid-sized businesses and to schools and universities for years. He knows what works in practice and what is only a demo. At WhiteSquirrel.ai he leads every assessment himself, meeting owners in person around Western North Carolina, and makes sure every prescription is something an owner can actually use. When SIGNLD is the right fit for a client, Chris says so and explains why. When it isn't, he recommends something else.
