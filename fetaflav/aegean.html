Our Story

Made with love & tradition

Feta Flav started rolling as a food truck in 2019 with one goal: serve the best Greek food in Western North Carolina. Today you'll find us cooking inside Oklawaha Brewing in downtown Hendersonville.

Pair a gyro with a local pint, split a dip duo with friends, and save room for baklava. Every plate is built the way we like to eat: generous, fresh and loaded with feta.
